INTERSTELLAR

Interstellar transforms the cold, silent vacuum of space into a deeply emotional canvas where the vastness of the cosmos collides with the fragility of human love. As the Endurance sails past the churning accretion disk of the black hole Gargantua and slips through the quiet geometry of a wormhole, the film reminds us that space is not merely an empty abyss of dead rock and starlight, but a mirror for our own time, memory, and longing. It captures how seconds can stretch into decades through the cruel physics of relativity, yet proves that a father's promise to his daughter can ripple across dimensions to span light-years.
